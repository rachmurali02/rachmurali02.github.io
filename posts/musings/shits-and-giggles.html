1 min read

Shits and Giggles

There’s a part of me that feels whole just hearing my friends and me laugh together. There’s no script, no structure — just chaos and connection. Somehow, even as we poke fun at each other with no rhyme or reason, it feels deeply fulfilling.

We talk, we jab, we banter. We lose our balance and fall into fits of laughter.
It’s ruckus. It’s madness. It’s directionless — and yet, it’s complete.

Inside jokes, fossilized from midnight moments and old summers, bloom like wildflowers: untamed, unexplained, rooted in familiarity and comfort.

It’s competitive — a ping-pong match with many players, no scoreboard, and only laughter filling the room. The gaslighter stokes the fire just to see sparks fly. The martyr rotates, armed with fiery comebacks. And the rest of us hop between sharp one-liners, quick wit, and that disastrous mix of unhinged energy and specific knowledge

It’s playful. It’s affectionate.
It’s nostalgic. It's alive and loud.
