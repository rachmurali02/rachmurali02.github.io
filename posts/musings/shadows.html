1 min read

Shadows

There’s a furtive shadow at your feet.
It follows you around. It doesn't block you. It stays hopeful
It sleeps in the dark just like you should
It morphs itself to the many vagaries of time
But every now and then, a light - soft and sudden - brings it alive
You stop walking- it does too.
But, you need to keep treading, and as you do, more shadows form
The shadows of the past carrying the hues of regret

Ignore it - It stays
Disregard it - It haunts
Embrace it - It heals

As I continue to form more shadows,
I know its not the end
Its a new life
