Poem

Apr 10, 2025

1 min read

In my space

It’s not a hush. It’s not a whimper. It’s not the drop of a pin.
It’s vacuous. It’s hollow. It’s jarring.
I hum, I type, I chatter
To forget that I’m alone.
My voice echoes back from digital walls,
Convincing me that I exist.
The glow of a screen—superior to any happy light—
And the vents huff and puff rhythmically.
The chair squeaks,
The floor remembers the anxious pat-pat.
Nothing waits to check on silence.
Breath halts to grieve a sigh,
A racing heartbeat in tandem with the clacking of keys.
Somewhere, I look around for a sound from my real space.
It was a knock at the other apartment.
I continue to clack away—unabated, almost wishful—
Hoping it might lead me away from my own stale voice
