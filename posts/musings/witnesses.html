Witnesses

Sometimes I feel like we live to find witnesses in our lives. A witness to see our groggy mornings, someone to notice the tangles in your hair, the haphazard nature of your day. An odd friendship where they are not actively fiddling with the moment, but someone who surrenders themselves to the chaos of it. Someone who embarks on a journey with you to detangle every moment passively, remarking on the ludicrousness of it, not trying to change the course of it, but is blissfully present in its essence.
