Reflecting on a sound

“Echo” is such a funny word. It's a repetition, but not the actual sound. You can ‘echo’ emotions, thoughts and feelings, but you can never be the emotion, thought or feeling. It’s a reflection — close to the actual, but not quite. It feeds off of the real world, almost as if giving you a mirror to yourself, and a lens into your heart. It’s a residue, the only lingering remnants of the “what ifs” that reverberate with time. Its one of the few things we welcome gladly even though it's delayed. We listen for it when the room grows quiet reminding us of the noise that once filled it. We crave its return to resurrect memories, and we pray it never dies because sometimes that's all that's left.
