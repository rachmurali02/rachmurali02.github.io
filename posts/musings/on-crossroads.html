Reflection

Jan 4, 2019

1 min read

On crossroads

2017-18 marked years of surprises, irritabilities, idiosyncracies, and boundless joy! I have been blessed with opportunities. There were unprecedented failures at many things, but ironically, those setbacks made my years much more riveting and real. I could not have hoped for better 17s and 18s of my life :D

Over the course of these rather busy two years, I met new friends who had similar and bizarre interests, friends who could laugh with me at my impossible attempts to find the ionization constant of an insoluble polyprotic drug, (my brutally damaged chemistry work) and cry with me when we barely passed our Gatsby-Outsider analyses. I am not sure whether it was our unanimous contempt towards Fitzgerald's rather cryptic language that brought us together, but we stood by each other.

I talked with old friends who were satisfied and at peace with themselves, almost making me a bit envious of their eternally blissful states, and caught up with untouched souls who grounded me in my rather cheerful talkative self, dreaming of a world filled with food and cricket.

Thank you for being instrumental in my two years, for without you I couldnt have enjoyed my best concert these two years!
